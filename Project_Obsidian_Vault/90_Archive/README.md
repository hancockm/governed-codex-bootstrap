The archive preserves superseded, closed, or historical material with enough
provenance to explain and, where permitted, reconstruct prior states.

## Admission

Archive only after current navigation points to the replacement or terminal
disposition. Record original path, owner, reason, date or commit, replacement,
and any restoration conditions.

## Boundaries

- An archive is not more authoritative than current canonical material.
- Archival is not deletion and does not erase unresolved residue.
- Generated continuity or coordination records follow their owning tools.
- Another owner's material requires that owner's disposition.
- Do not move files merely to make an active area look tidy.

## Restoration

Restore from preserved source and manifests, validate hashes and links, and
record why the material is current again. Never reconstruct missing history
from recollection.
