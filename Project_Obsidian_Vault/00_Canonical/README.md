This area contains current accepted project doctrine. Its documents are
separate because they answer different questions.

## Responsibilities

- [Core Thesis.md](Core%20Thesis.md): governing claims and why the project exists;
- [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md): stable components, planes, owners, and boundaries;
- [SPEC.md](SPEC.md): observable behavior and non-negotiable contracts;
- [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md): ordered, authorized capability gates;
- [Capability Registry.md](Capability%20Registry.md): narrative entry to machine-readable maturity;
- [Current State.md](Current%20State.md): evidence-backed current maturity projection.

## Promotion Boundary

Research records, critiques, tests, and transcripts are evidence. Core may
promote a conclusion only after resolving its source, uncertainty, owner,
authorization, and required verification. A useful proposal does not update
canonical truth automatically.

## Change Discipline

Change only the documents whose responsibilities actually move. A runtime
implementation may require Spec, Roadmap, Current State, or registry evidence
without changing the Thesis. A new governing claim may require all of them.
Record an explicit no-update reason when a substantial disposition leaves
canonical material unchanged.

Preserve historical commits and receipts. Do not rewrite prior states to make
the current decision appear inevitable.
